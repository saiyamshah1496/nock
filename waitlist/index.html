The problem

Postgres DDL can take strong locks. On busy systems those locks cascade — blocking queries, migrations, and sometimes entire services. It’s a pattern many teams hit as data grows and release velocity increases.

What Nock does

Nock puts governance in front of risky schema changes. It evaluates proposed DDL and enforces an approve/block gate before it ships — helping your org build safe, consistent habits around schema evolution.

How it thinks

  • Size-aware checks — considers table/object sizes when flagging risk.
  • lock_timeout discipline — prevents untamed locks from stalling production.
  • Plain‑language guidance — pragmatic for platform/SRE leads, not theory.
